How it works from distress call to dispatch plan

01
Capture the situation
Distress calls, ambulance locations and road travel times are gathered for every call.
02
Model it as a QUBO
Every choice becomes a yes/no switch: does ambulance A take call 1? Travel time adds cost, and broken rules (one ambulance per call, each ambulance used once) add heavy penalties.
03
Fit it to the hardware
The state-level problem is split by zone so each piece fits the Ising machine. Fitting the problem to the hardware is a big part of the engineering.
04
Solve with light
On Quanfluence's Ising machine, each spin is a pulse of light in a fibre loop. The pulses interact and settle into the lowest-energy state: the best plan.
05
Decode & dispatch
The spins are turned back into a dispatch plan: the right ambulance and the fastest route for every call in the zone.
BUILT TO EXTEND

The model can include rules such as matching the patient to the right hospital.

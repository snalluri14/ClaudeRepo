## Notes before you present

- **Accuracy of claims.** The video says *quantum-inspired* and *Ising machine*, not "quantum computer". Keep the spoken words the same.
- **Numbers.** The 8.9 × 10¹² figure is real arithmetic for the scenario shown on screen: 6 incidents need 12 vehicles, chosen from 18 (18!/6!). The factory example (21 → 10 changeovers) is labelled **illustrative** on screen. Don't present either as a measured field result.
- **Names.** The video names the Government of Andhra Pradesh and Quanfluence. It does **not** name Kenvue; the manufacturing scene is generic. Confirm approval for both names before 3 October. You can name Kenvue verbally only if they approve.

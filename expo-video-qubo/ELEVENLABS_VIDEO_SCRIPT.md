# ElevenLabs video script: Arohak × Quanfluence, 112 use case (about 2 min 50 s)

Copy one scene card at a time into ElevenLabs.
- **Clip prompt** goes into video generation. Start every prompt with the style line below.
- **Voice** goes into text-to-speech.
- **Overlay** is added in the editor (ElevenLabs Studio timeline, CapCut or Canva), using the real logo files in `logos/`.

**Logo files:** `logos/arohak_logo.png` · `logos/quanfluence_logo.png` (also available as SVG).
Both logos are **dark blue on a transparent background**. On the dark video scenes, place them on a **white rounded card** so they stay visible. The opening and closing scenes use a light background for this reason.

**Style line (start every clip prompt with this, except scenes 0a, 0b and 13):**
> Cinematic 16:9, dark navy background, glowing cyan and soft gold accents, clean minimal motion-graphics style, smooth slow camera movement, high-end tech keynote look. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermarks.

**Voice:** Indian English, male, 40s, calm, confident and warm; a senior technology leader presenting to government officials; measured pace, never salesy. Settings: Stability medium-high · Similarity high · Style low.

---

### 0a · Arohak logo (4 s)
- **Clip prompt:** Clean bright white studio background with a very soft light-grey gradient. A gentle horizontal light sweep passes across the centre, with faint drifting particles. Leave the centre empty for a logo. No text, no logos.
- **Voice:** "Arohak Technologies."
- **Overlay:** `arohak_logo.png`, centred, fading in.

### 0b · Partnership (4 s)
- **Clip prompt:** Clean bright white background. A thin blue line draws across the middle from left to right, with two soft glowing points at its ends. Calm, premium and symmetrical, with empty space on the left and right for logos. No text, no logos.
- **Voice:** "In partnership with Quanfluence."
- **Overlay:** Arohak logo (left) · `quanfluence_logo.png` (right) · text **"in partnership with Quanfluence"**.

### 1 · Calls across the state (8 s)
- **Clip prompt:** Night. Overhead view of a dark stylised map of a South Indian state with faint glowing roads. Red location pins pop up one after another across different regions, each pulsing softly, until a dozen glow across the map. Tense, urgent mood.
- **Voice:** "Every minute, distress calls light up across the state. A cardiac emergency. A road accident. A woman in labour. A child with a high fever."
- **Overlay:** **Every minute, distress calls light up across the state.**

### 2 · Three decisions (12 s)
- **Clip prompt:** Overhead dark map covered in pulsing red pins. From every pin at once, three small glowing icons ripple outward: an ambulance, a winding road and a hospital building. Slow push-in.
- **Voice:** "For every call, three decisions, made in seconds. Which ambulance. Which route. And which hospital."
- **Overlay:** **For every call: which ambulance? Which route? Which hospital?**

### 3 · Right hospital (15 s)
- **Clip prompt:** Camera zooms into one red pin on the dark map. Two hospital buildings nearby: the closer one glows soft red and dims; a farther one, marked with a heart symbol, glows green. A cyan path draws from the pin past the near hospital to the farther green one.
- **Voice:** "Take the cardiac call. The nearest hospital isn't always the right one. A heart patient needs cardiac care... not a children's hospital. And our model can include rules like that."
- **Overlay:** **The nearest hospital is not always the right hospital.** · *The model can include rules such as matching the patient to the right hospital.* · small tag *illustrative*

### 4 · Complexity (20 s)
- **Clip prompt:** Slow zoom out over the dark state map. Dozens of red pins, ambulance icons and hospital icons appear, connected by many tangled, flickering lines of light. New pins keep appearing and the lines re-tangle, growing dense and chaotic.
- **Voice:** "And every new call changes the whole picture. Multiply every call by every ambulance, and every hospital... and the number of possible plans explodes."
- **Overlay:** **Every new call changes the whole plan.**

### 5 · Step 1: Yes/no switches (15 s)
- **Clip prompt:** An abstract grid of small glowing toggle switches floating in dark space, in rows and columns, each flipping between dim (off) and bright cyan (on) one by one. Clean, mathematical and elegant.
- **Voice:** "Step one. Arohak turns every choice into a simple yes-or-no switch. Does ambulance A take call one? Does ambulance B take call two?"
- **Overlay:** **① Every decision becomes a yes/no switch**

### 6 · Step 2: QUBO (15 s)
- **Clip prompt:** The glowing switch grid feeds streams of light into a single vertical energy meter. Small glowing rule tiles (a clock, an ambulance, a "one" symbol) snap onto the grid, then a faint dashed tile with a hospital symbol fades in. Some connections flash gold when penalised. The meter level drops.
- **Voice:** "Step two. We write it as a Kew-bo: one score, where lower is better. Travel time adds cost, and breaking a rule adds a heavy penalty. The model can include rules such as matching the patient to the right hospital."
- **Overlay:** **② QUBO: one cost score. Lower = better.**

### 7 · Step 3: Fit to hardware (15 s)
- **Clip prompt:** A dark stylised state map splits into several zones along glowing borders. Each zone lifts up, compresses into a neat cube of light points, and slides smoothly into a sleek glowing slot, like puzzle pieces fitting perfectly.
- **Voice:** "Step three. Fitting the problem to the hardware. We split the state into zones, so each piece fits the machine. This is a big part of the engineering."
- **Overlay:** **③ Split by zone so each piece fits the machine**

### 8 · Step 4: Optical Ising machine (15 s)
- **Clip prompt:** Close-up of a glowing coiled optical fibre loop in darkness. Bright pulses of light travel around the loop, flicker and interact, then gradually synchronise into a calm, stable rhythm. Scientific, beautiful and precise, with a laser-lab look.
- **Voice:** "Step four. Quanfluence's Ising machine. Each spin is a pulse of light, travelling in a fibre loop. The pulses interact, and settle into their lowest-energy state... and that state is the best plan."
- **Overlay:** **④ Each spin is a pulse of light in a fibre loop** · small Quanfluence logo on a white card

### 9 · Step 5: Decode (5 s)
- **Clip prompt:** Points of light fly out of the fibre loop and land on a dark map, forming clean cyan routes from ambulance icons to patient pins. Everything turns calm and green.
- **Voice:** "Step five. The spins become the dispatch plan: the right ambulance, and the fastest route."
- **Overlay:** **⑤ Spins → dispatch plan** · *Right ambulance · fastest route*

### 10 · Result: to the patient (15 s)
- **Clip prompt:** Split screen with two identical dark maps. On the left, a grey ambulance takes a long winding route. On the right, a cyan ambulance takes a direct route and arrives much earlier; its pin turns green. Leave space at the top of each half for clocks.
- **Voice:** "Tested on real one-oh-eight call data: today, help reaches the patient in twenty-two point six minutes on average. With our routing... nine point eight. That's thirteen minutes sooner."
- **Overlay:** Left **Current 108 · LIVE DATA · 22.6 min** · Right **Ising-machine routing · SIMULATED · 9.8 min** · badge **13 min sooner (−57%)**

### 11 · Result: into hospital care (15 s)
- **Clip prompt:** Two horizontal bars of light grow from left to right on a dark background. The top grey bar grows longer; the bottom bar, made of cyan and gold segments, stops noticeably shorter. A soft green glow highlights the gap.
- **Voice:** "And into hospital care: forty-seven point five minutes, instead of sixty point six. Even after we add thirty minutes of buffer to our own numbers."
- **Overlay:** **Call → hospital care: 60.6 vs 47.5 min** · badge **13 min sooner (−22%)** · footnote *Current 108: live data. Ising-machine routing: simulation on the same 108 calls, with 15 min at the scene and 15 min handover added to the simulated side only. Not yet deployed live.*

### 12 · Roadmap (8 s)
- **Clip prompt:** Three glowing circular nodes joined by a cyan dotted line on a dark background. The first fills green with a check-mark glow; the line travels on to the second (an ambulance icon) and then the third (police car, fire engine and ambulance together).
- **Voice:** "We started with ambulances. Next comes a live trial... and then all of one-one-two: police, fire, and ambulance, as one."
- **Overlay:** ✓ **Simulation on 108 data** → **Live 108 trial** → **112** · **Ambulance today. All of 112 next.**

### 13 · Closing (7 s)
- **Clip prompt:** Clean bright white background with slow-drifting soft blue and grey light particles and a gentle central glow, with empty space for two logos side by side and a line of text below. No text, no logos.
- **Voice:** "Arohak and Quanfluence. Quantum-inspired today. Quantum-ready tomorrow."
- **Overlay:** `arohak_logo.png` (left) × `quanfluence_logo.png` (right) · *Quantum-inspired today. Quantum-ready tomorrow.*
